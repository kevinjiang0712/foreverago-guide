## Perform the prompt, then let the interaction settle

For each rest opportunity, use a consistent routine:

1. Approach the marked bench, sofa, lounger, or lake-facing railing.
2. Adjust Alfred's position and camera angle if the prompt is difficult to reveal.
3. Activate its rest interaction: sitting, the nap, or Take In View.
4. Allow the rest animation and local dialogue to play out.
5. Leave the interaction normally and record that opportunity as complete.

Allowing the scene to finish is a practical way to avoid cutting the interaction short. It is not a requirement to wait a fixed, invented number of seconds. The achievement description does not specify a universal rest timer, so do not turn every seat into a stopwatch challenge.

Likewise, avoid inferring success from Alfred standing still. An idle character is not necessarily resting in the achievement's sense. The reliable distinction is whether you actually activated the rest interaction that the environment offered.

## Use a continuous route for the completion attempt

For an Old Bones attempt, complete the six rests in one continuous journey. This is the conservative route when the achievement asks for every opportunity across several locations. It avoids depending on assumptions about whether isolated chapter replays combine all previously missed rest interactions.
