Shopaholic is the optional shopping achievement at Karl's Pump 'n' Mart. The story needs supplies, while the achievement requires the complete shopping set. Gather everything before paying Milton; finishing the ordinary shopping objective is not the same as buying all the optional products.

## Buy all eight listed products

| Product | Why it belongs in your basket |
| --- | --- |
| Windshield wipers | Required road-trip supply; look toward the far right of the store |
| Road atlas | Required road-trip supply; near the toilet-paper area and old photograph |
| Cookies | Story shopping item, on the opposite side from the wipers |
| Chips | Extra product for the full shopping set |
| Canned goods | Extra product for the full shopping set |
| Air freshener | Extra product for the full shopping set |
| Shirt | Extra product for the full shopping set |
| Sticker | Extra product for the full shopping set |

The list uses product types from the release guide. It is not a claim that every shop prop is purchasable, or that you need multiple copies of each product. This guide has not established exact shelf coordinates for the five optional products; inspect their item prompts while sweeping the store rather than treating decorative packaging as another requirement.

## Finish the shopping pass before checkout

1. Speak to Milton and take the red basket near the entrance.
2. Collect the cookies, wipers and road atlas for the story objective.
3. Sweep the shop for chips, canned goods, the air freshener, shirt and sticker. Check each interaction rather than leaving as soon as the story supplies are ready.
4. If also attempting Patience Of A Saint, let Milton finish his sales pitches for the atlas and wipers before taking those items. That is a separate requirement from shopping quantity.
5. Return to the counter after collecting the complete list and finish the payment sequence.
6. Check Shopaholic in your platform's achievement list before marking it complete in the [completion tracker](content:forever-ago-completion-tracker).

For the story transition after checkout, use the [full walkthrough](content:forever-ago-walkthrough). For the store's camera opportunity, use the [Milton selfie guide](content:forever-ago-selfie-achievement).

## Keep these gas-station goals separate

| Goal | Actual completion check |
| --- | --- |
| Shopaholic | Buy the complete product set and verify the platform unlock |
| Before I Forget Myself | Finish the gas-station story sequence |
| Patience Of A Saint | Allow Milton's item speeches to finish |
| Chrono Selfie | Photograph Alfred and Milton together in selfie mode |
| Old Bones | Use both gas-station rest opportunities; shopping does not count |

The [achievement hub](content:forever-ago-achievements) lists their distinct conditions and Epic XP. A photograph of merchandise, an interaction with the basket, or a normal story completion should not be used as evidence that all products were purchased.

## If Shopaholic did not unlock

Check the five optional product types first, then confirm that you actually paid. Compare against your platform list rather than relying only on the story objective disappearing. If the chapter has ended, [chapter select](content:forever-ago-chapter-select) provides a targeted replay route after story completion. Replay the complete shopping and payment sequence; this guide has not verified that purchases from separate visits combine, or that an earlier purchase can be carried into a new attempt.
