World’s Largest Mosaic Pumpkin Collection takes place at Alfred’s home in the epilogue, after the main mountain sequence and credits. Inspect the small mosaic pumpkins around the house before finishing dinner and the closing Doggo interaction.

The giant pumpkin outside Karl’s Pump ’N’ Mart is a different task. Its petals unlock a [gas-station vignette](content:forever-ago-memory-locations); it is not one of this seven-object home set.

## Seven pumpkin locations in the epilogue

| Check | Location | Search detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Audrey’s office | Inspect the small mosaic pumpkin in the office |
| 2 | Hallway windowsill | Check the sill rather than only objects at floor level |
| 3 | Bathroom, behind the door | Enter far enough to inspect the space the open door obscures |
| 4 | Bathroom, beside the bathtub | Search this separately from the behind-door pumpkin |
| 5 | Shelf above the stairwell | Look upward from the stairs and adjust the viewing angle |
| 6 | Living-room dresser | Inspect the decorative object on the dresser |
| 7 | Dining table in the living area | Check the table before moving on to prepare dinner |

The count and positions follow the release achievement guide. Room naming can differ in player descriptions; the fixed furniture landmarks are more useful than calling every space a separate formal room.

## Make one pass through the house

1. Clear the office and hallway sill before going downstairs.
2. Check both bathroom positions. Finding one pumpkin does not clear the room.
3. From the stairwell, look up at the shelf and adjust Alfred’s position until the inspect prompt appears.
4. In the living/dining area, check the dresser and table separately.
5. Confirm the achievement in your platform’s list, then finish the kitchen and dog sequence.

The release playthrough searches the home at [4:46:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t9P-yBLXF0E&t=17216s) and records the achievement at [4:55:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t9P-yBLXF0E&t=17707s). Use that footage to compare the scene, especially if the high shelf’s prompt is awkward.

## If one pumpkin is missing

Begin with the two bathroom checks and the shelf above the stairs. These are easy to combine mentally with a nearby discovery or miss because of a door and viewing angle. Next, separate the living-room dresser from the dining table; they are two objects in the same larger living space.

Activate the actual inspection rather than just taking a camera picture of the decoration. Search accessible interior positions before trying to reach pumpkins visible outside the house; the documented completion route uses the seven interior positions above.

If the epilogue has ended, check [chapter select](content:forever-ago-chapter-select) for a focused return. Do not erase a completed save merely to repeat the house search. Record the seven checks in the [completion tracker](content:forever-ago-completion-tracker).
