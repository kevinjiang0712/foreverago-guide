## Steam features, controllers and languages

The official listing identifies **Steam Achievements, Steam Cloud and Family Sharing**. Steam Cloud support does not establish save transfers between the Steam edition and PS5 or Xbox. If moving between platforms is essential to your purchase, do not assume those saves are interchangeable.

The store's controller information lists full Xbox controller support and support for PS4 and PS5 controllers, including Bluetooth. That does not establish PC support for every third-party controller or for DualSense trigger effects. The [Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1215940/Forever_Ago/?cc=us&l=english) is the reference for the PC edition's supported devices.

**English is the only language marked for full audio.** Interface and subtitles are listed in 13 languages: English, French, Italian, German, Spanish (Spain), Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Spanish (Latin America), and Traditional Chinese. Choose based on subtitle and interface support if you need a language other than English; those checkmarks do not indicate localized voice acting.

## What you are buying

Forever Ago is a single-player narrative road trip from Third Shift, published by Annapurna Interactive. You follow Alfred north, explore places on foot, examine objects, meet people, and use his instant camera to record the journey and solve small environmental puzzles. This makes the camera part of exploration rather than simply a photo mode. [Official publisher overview](https://annapurnainteractive.com/games/forever-ago).
