Scallyrat Roberts asks you to learn what led to the resort's collapse. The release achievement route identifies eight notes and newspaper clippings at Captain Marley & The Buccaneers' Retreat. Read them before taking the story boat. Photographing a notice or completing the resort's required journal tasks does not replace its reading interaction.

## Identify the eight written clues

The images below are a visual checklist, numbered to match the cited release guide. The numbers are not in-game collectible IDs and do not promise the shortest walking order. Use the pictured furniture and interaction prompt to distinguish similar papers; follow the [resort walkthrough](content:forever-ago-walkthrough) for access to the area's story spaces.

### 1. Graffiti-covered pillar

The first reference shows a red paper on a round, graffiti-covered pillar. Two wooden benches sit behind and to the right of the pillar. Approach the paper and use its interaction; merely reading graffiti nearby is a different action.

### 2. Wooden walkway floor

Look down on the narrow wooden walkway with a wall on one side and a metal handrail on the other. The note lies on the boards beside loose pieces of wood. The reference clearly shows a Read Note prompt at floor level.

![Scallyrat Roberts clue 2: wooden walkway floor](asset:scallyrat-note-2)

### 3. Counter with pirate posters

Find the paper on a dark counter with pirate posters across its front. Tape and small objects sit beside the sheet, with a cardboard box farther along the counter. Aim the interaction at the paper rather than the poster art.

![Scallyrat Roberts clue 3: counter with pirate posters](asset:scallyrat-note-3)

### 4. Trestle table by the windows

The next reference shows a paper on a long table supported by red trestle legs. Tall windows, plants, a yellow spirit level and a roll of tape distinguish this table from the other desks.

![Scallyrat Roberts clue 4: trestle table by the windows](asset:scallyrat-note-4)

### 5. Storage shelving

In the storage area, examine the paper attached to the shelving among cardboard boxes. A hand truck stands in front of the shelves and a colorful pirate poster is on the adjacent wall. The Read Note prompt is on the shelf, not the trolley.

![Scallyrat Roberts clue 5: storage shelving](asset:scallyrat-note-5)

### 6. Workshop bench

Check the workbench beneath a board of hanging tools. The paper is beside a red cable reel, a pencil and small dark objects; part of an arcade screen is visible at the right edge of the reference. Use the note prompt on the tabletop.

![Scallyrat Roberts clue 6: workshop bench](asset:scallyrat-note-6)

### 7. Desk beneath the Captain Marley logo

A paper lies on the desk below the Captain Marley & Buccaneers logo. Nearby folders, loose papers and a desk fan help distinguish this workstation. Inspect the highlighted sheet rather than assuming any stack of papers is collectible.

![Scallyrat Roberts clue 7: desk beneath the captain marley logo](asset:scallyrat-note-7)

### 8. Wall-mounted newspaper

The eighth reference shows a newspaper clipping on a framed wall noticeboard beside a door. Its interaction reads Read Newspaper Article. A newspaper is part of this written-evidence set even though it does not use the Read Note wording.

![Scallyrat Roberts clue 8: wall-mounted newspaper](asset:scallyrat-note-8)

## Finish the reading pass before the boat

1. Follow the resort's required route to open its accessible areas and obtain the bolt cutter.
2. Use the eight visual references to inspect the actual papers and newspaper. Activate each reading prompt and let its text open.
3. Keep separate notes on which landmarks you have checked. Reading the same sheet twice is not a ninth distinct clue.
4. Before boarding, check Scallyrat Roberts in your own platform list. Mark the achievement only after confirming the unlock.
5. Finish any other resort cleanup, including [Marley's bobblehead](content:forever-ago-marleys-bobblehead), [Guardian Lord](content:forever-ago-guardian-lord), and the [three resort memories](content:forever-ago-memory-locations).

The [completion tracker](content:forever-ago-completion-tracker) has a Scallyrat Roberts achievement row that links back here. That row records the completed achievement; it does not automatically detect which of these eight papers you read.

## If the achievement remains locked

Compare the physical landmarks again. The floor note and storage-shelf note are easy to confuse with props because they are not both on a conventional desk. Also check the framed newspaper, whose interaction wording differs from the notes.

Do not substitute a petal memory, the bobblehead, a souvenir or an ordinary resort sign for one of the eight reading prompts. Each belongs to its own objective. If you already departed, use [chapter select](content:forever-ago-chapter-select) for focused cleanup after the story. This guide has not independently established whether readings from different replays merge, so a fresh attempt should inspect the complete set and verify the platform result.
