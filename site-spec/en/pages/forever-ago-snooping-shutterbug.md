Snooping Shutterbug rewards photographs of the curious objects in Alfred’s room. Do the sweep while Audrey still controls the opening camera. The calendar leads toward Alfred calling from the window, so leave it until the other room details have been checked.

## Bedroom sweep before the calendar

Start with the manual, then work across one part of the room at a time. The following candidates are grouped from player reports; decorative naming and the exact recognized set are not an official object inventory.

| Search zone | Photo candidates |
| --- | --- |
| Bed and footboard | Teddy, model volcano, nearby comic or foreground object |
| Desk wall | Family display, small paper and pink pen below it |
| Floor and study corner | Books, colored light, cushions and backpack supplies |
| Closet side | Clothing/cards display, nearby books and plant |
| Wall decorations | Falls sign, team banner, movie and music posters, medal pair |
| High and right-side details | Clock figure and the clock above the doorway |
| Final check | Manual if not already photographed, then calendar |

Keep the desk paper/pen and high clock in the sweep: commenters specifically reported them as their final missing subjects. Isolate adjacent displays when one broad shot gives no useful feedback. A spoken reaction helps identify a recognized subject, but silence is not definitive proof that a candidate does not count.

## Confirm the room task before the window portrait

Wait for the current reaction to finish before changing targets. Check Snooping Shutterbug in your platform’s list after the sweep; a photograph being captured is not the same signal as the complete set unlocking.

Release footage shows the unlock at [13:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t9P-yBLXF0E&t=812s), just before the Alfred portrait progresses the opening. This is a useful scene-order reference, not a requirement to reach a timestamp or photograph the room at the video’s speed.

Once the achievement is recorded, photograph Alfred through the window and continue the [Woolson Falls photo route](content:forever-ago-chapter-1-photos).

## If Snooping Shutterbug remains locked

While control remains in the room, recheck the small desk stationery, foreground comic, individual posters and clocks. Change the angle enough to separate the intended subject from its neighbors. Do not assume several photos of the teddy compensate for a missing object.

If you have already left, keep it as a focused opening replay task after finishing the story. Use the [chapter-select guide](content:forever-ago-chapter-select) rather than starting a new game without checking the replay menu. The site has not independently tested whether partial object photos from separate attempts combine.

This task is separate from the total-photo milestones. Use [camera achievements](content:forever-ago-camera-achievements) for 25 photos, 50 photos and ten selfies, and [the completion tracker](content:forever-ago-completion-tracker) to retain your room check.
