## An independent resource for the journey

Forever Ago Wiki is an independent English-language guide site maintained by Kevin at https://www.foreverago-guide.wiki/. It covers the narrative adventure developed by Third Shift and published by Annapurna Interactive. The site is not an official game website and is not affiliated with either company.

The guides organise information around the task you are trying to complete: using the camera, choosing journal photos, finding memories, pursuing achievements, replaying chapters, or understanding a story scene. The review section also explains pacing and reported game length so you can decide how much time to allow.

## How we approach guide content

Articles draw on publicly available game information, published walkthroughs, and relevant reference material. We prioritise official information and check specific claims against the available evidence. We do not claim a personal playthrough, first-hand achievement completion, or developer access where that has not occurred.

A published guide is not a guarantee that every game version, platform, or individual save behaves identically. When exact bindings, counts, or replay rules cannot be established, the text should say so rather than supply a guess. Story interpretations are explanations of the scenes, not statements of an undisclosed developer intention.

## Spoilers and illustrations

Story explanations and late-game walkthroughs contain spoilers. Their opening text identifies that scope so you can decide whether to continue. Photography and achievement pages may also reveal locations or optional discoveries.

Gameplay screenshots illustrate the article body. Promotional artwork provides site imagery. The game, its characters, artwork, and associated names belong to their respective rights holders; displaying an illustration does not make this an official resource.
