## Scope and contact

Effective date: October 12, 2026.

This policy describes data use on the independent Forever Ago guide site at https://www.foreverago-guide.wiki/, maintained by Kevin. For privacy questions, contact `kevinjiang0712@gmail.com`.

## Google Analytics 4

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand page visits, navigation, and site use. Its measurement technology can use first-party cookies and collect device or browser information, page activity, and identifiers. Google states that Analytics does not log or store IP addresses. We use the reports to understand which guides are useful and where the site needs improvement.

Read [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and its [Analytics data safeguards](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245?hl=en) for information about the service. Google also provides an [Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Microsoft Clarity

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with the site through behavioural metrics, heatmaps, and session replay. This can capture interaction patterns such as clicks and scrolling using cookies and other tracking technology. The insights help identify confusing navigation and improve page usability. Microsoft also processes the information according to its own policies.

Read the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement) for details about Microsoft’s collection and use of data.

## Adsterra advertising

The site displays advertising through Adsterra. Ad-serving technology and participating providers may receive technical information, including browser or device details, IP addresses, page or referrer information, and ad interactions. They may use cookies or similar technology to deliver advertising, measure performance, and detect invalid activity.

Advertisements and their destination sites are third-party services. Visiting an advertised site is subject to that site’s policies. Read [Adsterra’s privacy policy](https://adsterra.com/privacy-policy/) for information about its service and data practices.

If you contact us, we receive the email address, message, and any information you choose to include. We use that information to review and respond to the enquiry. Send only what is necessary, and avoid passwords, payment information, or other sensitive details.

Provider settings and the Google opt-out tool offer additional controls. Browser controls do not necessarily remove information already processed by a provider.
