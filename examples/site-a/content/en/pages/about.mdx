## Why this site exists

<PolicyValue field="siteName" /> is an independent, fan-made guide for <PolicyValue field="gameName" />. It is not operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed by the game developer, publisher, or platform holder.

The site exists to make useful guides, references, and discovery paths easier to find. Game names, artwork, trademarks, and other protected materials remain the property of their respective owners.

## How guides are researched

Sources are prioritized in this order:

1. Official announcements, patch notes, manuals, and store pages.
2. Reproducible observations from the current game version.
3. Screenshots, recordings, and other media that readers can inspect.
4. Community reports used as leads until stronger evidence is available.

Version-sensitive claims should name the applicable version. Unknown values stay unverified instead of being filled with estimates.

## Evidence and editorial judgment

Facts, recommendations, and community findings serve different purposes. Guides should distinguish direct evidence from editorial judgment, identify material limits, and link to the strongest available source.

A published page can still be revised when the game changes or better evidence appears.

## AI-assisted editorial work

Automated tools may help organize research, compare drafts, or identify missing checks. They are not treated as sources. Factual claims still require review against the cited material, and the site does not invent credentials, testing, or official approval.

## Corrections and accountability

Send corrections, rights requests, or material errors to <PolicyContact />. Include the page URL, the disputed claim, the relevant game version, and a source that can be checked.

## Funding and advertising

Advertising is shown only when an advertising provider is explicitly configured. Paid placements must be labeled and kept separate from editorial conclusions. Any enabled provider and related data processing must also be disclosed in the Privacy Policy.
