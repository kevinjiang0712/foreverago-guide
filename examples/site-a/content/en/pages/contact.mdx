## What to contact us about

### Corrections and evidence

Report an inaccurate claim, broken citation, missing attribution, or material translation issue. Include the page URL and a source that can be checked.

### Game version updates

Flag information that changed after a patch. Include the platform, region when relevant, and the version or update date.

### Technical issues

Report broken links, unreadable layouts, accessibility problems, or features that fail to load. Include the page URL, device, browser, and the steps that reproduce the issue.

### Copyright and media

Rights holders may request attribution updates, corrections, or removal of protected material. Identify the item, the relevant URL, and your relationship to the work.

### Privacy

Use this channel for access, correction, deletion, or other privacy requests that apply under local law. Do not send sensitive identification unless it is necessary and specifically requested.

### Advertising and business

Clearly identify commercial proposals. Payment does not buy a favorable editorial conclusion, and sponsored material must be labeled.

## Helpful details

A useful message includes the affected URL, a concise description, the expected result, screenshots when needed, and links to primary evidence.

## Before you write

Do not send passwords, game save files, executables, private logs, payment details, or material you do not have permission to share. A link and a short explanation are usually enough.
