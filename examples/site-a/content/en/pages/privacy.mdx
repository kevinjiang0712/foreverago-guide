## Information that may be processed

### Hosting and security logs

The hosting provider may process request data such as IP address, requested URL, timestamp, browser information, referrer, and security signals to deliver the site, diagnose failures, and prevent abuse.

### Local browser storage

The site may store preferences such as language, appearance, local checklist progress, or privacy choices on the visitor's device. These values remain in the browser until they expire or are cleared.

### Messages you send

If you contact the site, the operator may receive your address, message, attachments, and any other information you choose to provide.

## Analytics, advertising, and embeds

Optional analytics, advertising, social, or media services load only when they are actually configured for this site. Before enabling a provider, the operator must update this policy with the provider name, purpose, data involved, and available controls.

The About, Contact, Privacy, and Terms pages do not display advertising placements. External sites opened from Community links apply their own privacy policies.

## Your choices and rights

You can clear local site data in your browser, block optional third-party requests with browser controls, and avoid sending information through the contact channel. Depending on applicable law, you may also request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection by contacting <PolicyContact />.

## Retention and security

Information should be kept only as long as needed for the stated purpose, legal obligations, dispute handling, or security. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet transmission or storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Children, external links, and changes

This site is a general game-information publication and is not designed to collect personal information from children. External links are governed by their own policies. Material changes to this policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
