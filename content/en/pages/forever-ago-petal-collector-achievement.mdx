Petal Collector asks for all seven optional vignettes. The route contains two at Karl’s Pump ’N’ Mart, two at Highway Star Diner and three at Captain Marley & The Buccaneers’ Retreat. Finish each local petal search and photograph the revealed memory.

## Understand what counts as one completed vignette

Use three states: petals incomplete, vignette unlocked, and memory revealed through the camera. The local fraction tracks the first stage. The unlock message makes the gathering point available. The camera response finishes the memory interaction.

| Stop | Names to complete | Route total afterward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gas station | Fun & Colors; The World’s Largest Mosaic Pumpkin | 2 |
| Diner | The Morning After; Seeking Shelter | 4 |
| Resort | Farewell Forever, Marley!; It’s Tradition; Responsible Adults | 7 |

Record these names in the <ContentLink id="forever-ago-completion-tracker">completion tracker</ContentLink>, rather than counting every ordinary flower or personal photograph.

## Keep optional vignettes separate from story flashbacks

The checkout memory, diner booth photograph, boat ride and lighthouse photograph advance the main story. They are not replacements for the named optional petal memories. Likewise, selecting pictures for the journal is a separate album action.

## Karl's Pump 'n' Mart: finish the first two memories

For Fun & Colors, search the swings, picnic table by the fence, then the blue slide and sandpit. Inspect the three local petals and finish the gathering-point photograph.

<Media id="body-images-forever-ago-petal-collector-achievement-fun-colors" />

For The World’s Largest Mosaic Pumpkin, search by the sculpture’s information board and accessible edge, follow its live counter, then photograph the memory. The giant roadside sculpture is unrelated to the <ContentLink id="forever-ago-mosaic-pumpkins">seven small home pumpkins</ContentLink> in the epilogue.

## Finish shopping without forgetting the outdoor search

Before paying and completing the store sequence, check both memories separately from purchases, Milton’s dialogue, his selfie and the two rests. Glimpse Into The Past confirms a first vignette; it does not establish that both gas-station memories are finished.

## Highway Star Diner: find The Morning After

The Morning After uses two pool-area petals: one around the steps and raised edge near the striped umbrella, the other beside the slide, green fence and pool rules board. Find the gathering point after both inspections and reveal the memory.

<Media id="body-images-forever-ago-petal-collector-achievement-morning-after" />

The <ContentLink id="forever-ago-petal-locations">ground-level petal route</ContentLink> gives the pool landmarks in more detail.

## Complete Seeking Shelter as a separate diner vignette

Inspect the petal at the exterior terrace stairs and the one by the glass entrance threshold, near the exit and membership signs. Their two-petal count belongs to Seeking Shelter, not the nearby pool memory. Finish its gathering-point camera action before the main booth photograph carries the story onward.

After the diner, the route should contain four completed optional vignettes.

## Resort: complete the final three vignettes

| Memory | Search pocket |
| --- | --- |
| Farewell Forever, Marley! | Graffiti area and pirate statue base |
| It’s Tradition | Pirate face cutout, low wall and nearby bench |
| Responsible Adults | Harbor building, waterfront platform and short steps |

<Media id="body-images-forever-ago-petal-collector-achievement-responsible-adults" />

The bolt cutter enables resort access, but obtaining it does not complete a vignette. Finish the shore-side searches and camera reveals before boarding the boat. The <ContentLink id="forever-ago-memory-locations">memory location guide</ContentLink> supplies recognition landmarks and local counts for each pocket.

## Use memory names to find the missing part of the set

Compare these seven entries with your notes: Fun & Colors, The World’s Largest Mosaic Pumpkin, The Morning After, Seeking Shelter, Farewell Forever, Marley!, It’s Tradition and Responsible Adults. A missing name identifies the region; a started name with no reveal identifies an unfinished local stage.

## Troubleshoot petals, photographs, and achievement progress separately

| What you see | Next action |
| --- | --- |
| Partial local fraction | Search the remaining ground-level landmark |
| Vignette unlocked | Find where the petals gather |
| Gathering point available | Complete the camera reveal |
| All seven reveals recorded, achievement still locked | Check the platform list and revisit the entry whose completion is uncertain |

Picture Perfect’s 50-photo requirement does not fill a missing vignette. Chapter select supports revisiting locations after the story, but this site has not independently tested partial petal-state merging across replays. Follow the live counter in the current scene.

## Confirm the complete set before ending the search

Confirm seven revealed vignettes in the two-two-three distribution and check Petal Collector on your platform. Keep the checklist until the unlock is recorded. A remembered story flashback or journal picture should not be used to fill an uncertain optional-memory entry.
