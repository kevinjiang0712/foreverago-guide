Use the tracker as a companion to the game, then confirm achievement unlocks in your platform’s own list. The checkmarks record what you tell the site you have done. They do not read your save, Steam account, Xbox profile or PlayStation trophies.

## Use the tracker during your journey

Choose your current stop and the collection you are working on. Search an achievement name or landmark when you have a specific missing action. Mark a task after performing it, then use its guide link when you need the location or prerequisites.

For vignettes, record the stage accurately: collect the local petals, find the gathering point and photograph the revealed memory. Do not mark a vignette finished at the first petal or the unlock message. The <ContentLink id="forever-ago-memory-locations">seven-memory guide</ContentLink> supplies the names and recognition landmarks; the <ContentLink id="forever-ago-petal-locations">petal route</ContentLink> focuses on ground-level searches.

## What each completion list means

| List | Complete an entry when | Confirmation outside this site |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Achievements | You performed the requirement and checked its unlock | Your own platform’s achievement or trophy list |
| Memories | Its petals and final camera reveal are complete | The local memory response has played |
| Rests | Alfred used the actual rest or Take In View prompt | The rest interaction happened, rather than simply visiting the landmark |

A counter of completed items is your checklist progress. It is not an official game completion percentage. Epic XP, Xbox gamerscore and PlayStation trophy grades are different systems, even where an achievement shares its name and action.

## Use local progress carefully

Progress belongs to the browser where you use the tool. Saving a check here does not make an in-game action persistent, and clearing this checklist does not erase a game save. Use the tool’s export and import controls when moving your notes between devices. Before resetting, export a copy if you want to retain the route you have already recorded.

Post-story chapter select supports focused cleanup of local tasks. The site has not independently tested how every cumulative requirement merges across replays; keep a continuous journey plan for Old Bones rather than marking a rest from a different run as proven cumulative progress.
