The six camera achievements need either a specific subject or a photograph count. **Do the room sweep first, the Milton selfie at the gas station, and the Doggo shot during the lighthouse sequence.** Work on 25 photos, 50 photos and 10 selfies throughout the trip. More scenery shots cannot replace a missed subject.

The conditions below follow the [official Epic achievement list](https://store.epicgames.com/achievements/forever-ago-c55775). Keep your own progress in the <ContentLink id="forever-ago-completion-tracker">completion checklist</ContentLink> and confirm each unlock in your platform's achievement list. Local checkmarks do not read your game save.

## Snooping Shutterbug needs the opening room's objects

**Stop: opening bedroom.** Photograph every curious object before advancing through the calendar and Alfred's window portrait.

- **Subject:** the room's separate curious objects. The teddy bear below is one target, not the whole set.
- **Mode:** normal, forward-facing camera.
- **Before leaving:** finish the <ContentLink id="forever-ago-snooping-shutterbug">bedroom sweep</ContentLink> and check the platform unlock before taking the scene-advancing window shot. The detailed guide separates visible candidates from a fully verified inventory; this site has not independently tested every object.

<Media id="body-images-forever-ago-camera-achievements-bedroom" />

**Picture check:** center the teddy bear on the bed, as shown through the camera. Photograph the other room subjects separately. Mark Snooping Shutterbug in the <ContentLink id="forever-ago-completion-tracker">completion checklist</ContentLink> only after checking the unlock.

## Chrono Selfie needs Alfred and Milton in the same shot

**Stop: Karl's Pump 'n' Mart.** Take a selfie with Milton, the cashier.

- **Subject:** Alfred and Milton together in the frame.
- **Mode:** selfie. A normal portrait of Milton does not match the condition.
- **Before leaving:** reposition if Milton is outside the frame, take the shared selfie and check Chrono Selfie on your platform.

<Media id="body-images-forever-ago-camera-achievements-milton-selfie" />

**Picture check:** Alfred fills the foreground and Milton is visible behind him. This is the shared selfie framing to check, rather than a picture of Alfred alone. See the <ContentLink id="forever-ago-selfie-achievement">selfie walkthrough</ContentLink>, then mark the action in the <ContentLink id="forever-ago-completion-tracker">completion checklist</ContentLink>.

## Photograph Doggo during the bird's-nest sequence

**Stop: Winslow Lighthouse.** It's A Bird, It's A Plane, It's Doggo requires the dog instead of the nest during that photography sequence.

- **Subject:** Doggo on the ground; lower the framing from the nest.
- **Mode:** normal camera, not selfie.
- **Before leaving:** take the dog photograph in this sequence and check the named achievement. The earlier diner dog request is a separate story action.

Use the <ContentLink id="forever-ago-dog-photo">dog-photo guide</ContentLink> to distinguish the two scenes, then record the lighthouse task in the <ContentLink id="forever-ago-completion-tracker">completion checklist</ContentLink>.

## Build the photo milestones during normal exploration

These totals have no named chapter or unique-subject requirement in the official descriptions. The suggestions below are a convenient route, not mandatory locations.

### Focus Finder: take 25 photos

- **Action:** take 25 photographs while exploring and following camera prompts.
- **Check:** the journal contains selected pictures; its page count is not a shutter counter. Confirm Focus Finder in the platform list.
- **Next step:** use <ContentLink id="forever-ago-camera-controls">camera controls</ContentLink> if you cannot take a shot, then record the milestone in the <ContentLink id="forever-ago-completion-tracker">completion checklist</ContentLink>.

### Picture Perfect: take 50 photos

- **Action:** continue photographing until the 50-photo condition unlocks. A single completed story photo does not prove the total is met.
- **Check:** confirm Picture Perfect separately from the 25-photo achievement.
- **Next step:** compare the <ContentLink id="forever-ago-achievements">full achievement requirements</ContentLink> and mark the larger milestone in the <ContentLink id="forever-ago-completion-tracker">completion checklist</ContentLink>.

## Self-Portrait Photography: take 10 selfies

- **Action:** switch to selfie mode and take 10 selfies. Ten outward-facing portraits are a different action.
- **Check:** Alfred should appear in the selfie view. This total is separate from Chrono Selfie's Milton requirement.
- **Next step:** follow the <ContentLink id="forever-ago-selfie-achievement">selfie-mode guide</ContentLink>, confirm the platform unlock and mark it in the <ContentLink id="forever-ago-completion-tracker">completion checklist</ContentLink>.

## Missed a subject or still short on a count?

Identify the locked condition before repeating shots: subject, camera mode and count are different checks. After the story, <ContentLink id="forever-ago-chapter-select">chapter select</ContentLink> can return you to a missed local task. New Game can overwrite the current save. This site has not verified how every partial photo counter combines across chapter jumps, so do not assume a saved local checklist proves an in-game total.

Petal memories belong to <ContentLink id="forever-ago-petal-collector-achievement">Petal Collector</ContentLink>, not these six conditions. If a vignette is missing, complete its local search and gathering-point camera interaction rather than adding unrelated photographs.
