**Forever Ago is available on PS5 as a single-player adventure**, released on October 8, 2026. The PlayStation Store lists DualSense vibration support and marks trigger effects as required. These controller features do not establish a particular frame rate, resolution or performance mode.

To buy the PS5 version, use the [official PlayStation Store listing](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/product/UP2470-PPSA35316_00-FOREVERAGOGLOBAL/). Check the current price in your account’s region before paying; a PlayStation Plus purchase discount is not the same as inclusion in the Plus game catalog.

## What the PS5 listing confirms

The US store details below were checked on October 22, 2026.

| Feature | Listed support |
| --- | --- |
| Platform | PS5 |
| Players | 1 player |
| DualSense vibration | Supported |
| Trigger effects | Marked as required |
| Remote Play | Supported |
| PS5 and PS Portal cloud streaming | Supported only with a Premium subscription |
| Power saver | Supported |

The store’s platform label confirms a PS5 release. It does not confirm a PS4 edition. It also provides no verified frame-rate target, resolution, performance-mode selection or PS5 Pro enhancement in the checked listing. Use those limits when comparing versions rather than treating the controller-support labels as graphics specifications.

## Buying, Remote Play and cloud streaming

These are separate entries on the store page, so choose the one that matches how you intend to play.

- **Buying the game:** review the game’s current purchase price and regional listing. A discounted purchase still requires buying the game; a Plus discount alone does not establish that it is free with your subscription.
- **Remote Play:** the game is marked as supported. That label is separate from the Premium cloud-streaming notice and should not be used to infer the same subscription requirement.
- **Cloud streaming:** the listing specifically names PS5 and PS Portal and says Premium is required. Check the applicable service availability and account conditions before choosing this route.

The cloud-streaming label does not, by itself, confirm inclusion in the PlayStation Plus game catalog. If subscription access determines your purchase decision, check the entitlement shown for your own account.

## Voice and screen languages

The PS5 listing specifies **English voice audio**. It lists these 13 screen languages:

Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), English, French (France), German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Spanish and Spanish (Mexico).

A screen-language entry is not a promise of voice acting in that language. If you want translated text with English dialogue, use the screen-language list when checking whether this version meets your needs.

## The adventure you are buying

The [publisher’s description](https://annapurnainteractive.com/games/forever-ago) presents Alfred’s journey north as a single-player road trip. You explore environments, examine objects and meet people along the way. Alfred’s instant camera both records the journey and helps solve small environmental puzzles.
